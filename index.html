One checklist a day, for 75 days

Steady75 is an iPhone and iPad app for 75 day challenges. Choose Strict, Medium or Soft rules or write your own, tick off each day, add a progress photo and watch the 75-day grid fill up. There is no account, and your days stay on your device.

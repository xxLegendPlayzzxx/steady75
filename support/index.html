Support

Answers to the questions people ask most. If yours is not here, write to us.

How do I start a challenge?

Open the app and follow four short steps: choose Strict, Medium or Soft rules and add a task of your own if you like, choose what happens when a day is missed, pick a start date, and set a daily reminder. Set-up and day 1 are free.

What happens if I miss a day?

You choose during set-up. With "Start again from day 1", Today tells you which day was missed and offers to start again; the app never restarts by itself. With "Mark it missed and carry on", the day shows as missed on your grid and the challenge goes on.

I did the day but forgot to tick it

Yesterday stays open until the end of today, for things ticked after midnight. For an older day, open it from the grid on Progress and tick what you did.

Can I pause the challenge?

Yes, with Pro. In Rules, tap Pause for travel or illness. Paused days have no number and can't be missed, and your day number picks up where you left off when you resume.

How do I start again?

In Rules, tap Start again. Your earlier run is kept with all its days and photos, and anything you ticked today comes with you to day 1.

Where are my progress photos kept?

Only in the app on your device. The app keeps a copy of each photo you take or choose; your photo library is not changed. There is no account and no cloud copy, so deleting the app deletes your days and photos.

How do I add the widgets?

Touch and hold an empty part of your Home Screen until the apps jiggle, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Steady75. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then tap the space for widgets. Widgets are part of Steady75 Pro.

What is in Steady75 Pro?

Your own rules and as many tasks as you like, including counted ones; the progress photo timeline and side-by-side compare; Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets; a second reminder and an evening nudge on days you haven't finished; pause; share cards; and the history of earlier challenges. Set-up, the daily checklist with a progress photo, the 75-day grid, a daily reminder and starting again are free.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Steady75 and tap Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in and choose the purchase.

My reminders don't arrive

Check that notifications are allowed for Steady75 in the Settings app, under Notifications, and that the reminder is switched on in the app's own Settings. Reminders are scheduled on your device, so they arrive without an internet connection.

Is Steady75 part of another 75 day program?

No. Steady75 is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any other 75-day challenge program or its owners. Its rule sets are its own, and you can change them.

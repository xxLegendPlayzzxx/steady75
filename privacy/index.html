Privacy policy

Effective 21 November 2026

In short

Steady75 is made by Dillon Pinto. It has no account, no advertising and no tracking. Your challenge, your days and your progress photos stay on your device. This policy explains the few cases where information leaves your device.

Your days stay on your device

Your challenge, every tick and note, your progress photos, your settings and your reminders are stored only in the app on your device. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see them. Deleting the app removes them.

Progress photos

You take a photo with the camera or pick one with Apple's photo picker, so the app sees only the photo you choose. A copy is kept in the app; your photo library is not changed. Saving a share card asks for permission to add pictures to your library, and nothing else. Share cards include a photo only if you switch it on.

Reminders and widgets

Reminders are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent from a server. Widgets show your day number and today's progress, read from the app on your device.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. To confirm what you have bought and to restore it on your devices, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc., which receives an anonymous identifier created by the app, your purchase records (what was bought, when, and whether it renewed or was refunded) and basic technical information such as your country, device type and app version. It does not receive your name, email address, photos or days. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we do not do

No sign-up, no advertising, no analytics kits, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of personal information.

Reports from Apple

Apple may give us totals, such as how many people installed the app or how often it stopped working, if you have agreed to share analytics with app developers. These reports do not identify you.

Children

Steady75 is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Because we hold no account or profile for you, there is usually nothing for us to look up, correct or delete. If you write to us, we will have your email address and will use it only to reply. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information, and to complain to your data protection authority. To use any of these rights, including for the anonymous purchase identifier held by RevenueCat, email us.

Where information is handled

RevenueCat is based in the United States. Information sent to it is handled under its own policy and the safeguards it describes.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
